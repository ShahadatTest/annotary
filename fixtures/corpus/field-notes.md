## Day by day

**Monday.** Kivik to Vitemölla, 9 km. Dry, with a cold wind off the water. We recorded 41 markers, of
which three had faded past reading, and the handheld held a fix of under three metres in the open. The
apple-orchard section is not on the 2019 data at all: the path runs along the top edge of the orchard
now, since the owner replanted.

**Tuesday.** Vitemölla to Stenshuvud, 7 km, slower than it sounds. The diversion takes the path through
beech woodland, where the fix drops to about eight metres, so we walked it twice and averaged the
tracks. Two benches turned up that nobody had recorded, and one bench that we had recorded did not.

**Wednesday.** Rain. We spent the morning in the van reconciling tracks, and the afternoon on the
headland once it cleared. The cliff edge has retreated about a metre and a half since the last survey,
and the warning sign now stands closer to the edge than the thing it warns about.

**Thursday.** Stenshuvud to Simrishamn, 14 km, along the beach for much of it. Nothing to report except
that the path across the sand is wherever the last storm left it, and we should stop drawing it as
a confident solid line.

## What changes in the data

- The Vitemölla diversion replaces 1.4 km of path in the next data build.
- Two benches and a water tap get added, and one bench is gone. Somebody has burnt it.
- The cliff symbol at Stenshuvud needs a new sprite; see the [onboarding guide](onboarding.md) for
  who to ask about sprites.
